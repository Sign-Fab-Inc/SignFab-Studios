About this check

All drawing imports use inches. Checks engrave lines, joined curves, tube marks, and .250″ stud marks. Ø.125″ holes, other round through-holes, outside panel contours, letter-center cutouts, and lettering outside the parts stay gray.

Green = open contour, including C-shaped stud marks and tube rectangles with visible gaps (one piece or two end-gapped brackets). Red = closed stud contour, including joined segments and paths whose endpoints meet. Tube engraves are blue when selected and orange when unselected. Yellow = branched or uncertain engrave geometry. Closed tube engraves count under Closed Marks. Other part contours stay gray.

Tube marks must be sharp-cornered rectangles (any rotation) inside a part outline, with nothing but round holes drawn inside them, and a width that matches one of the tube sizes listed above.

Open paths can still be cut if the laser is set to cut them. Confirm the stud marks use the engraving operation in your laser software.

DXF: ASCII 2D vectors. Diameter tolerance: ±.003″. Endpoint connection tolerance: .0000001″. Production-file validation is still pending.
